# 160.mss

Marketing Specs:

* Goals:

- Use existing 120 shell
- Open System:
  - Ability to plug in Multibus and VME cards
  - Sufficient number of slots
  - Sufficient Power

* System configurations:

Smallest (5 slots)			70 Amp

	Single Board CPU		12 Amp
	Memory Expansion + SCSI		8  Amp
	Color Frame Buffer		20 Amp
	Graphics Processor		20 Amp
	Spare (e.g. Sky Floating Point)	10 Amp

Medium (7 slots)			80 Amp

	CPU				12 Amp
	Memory Expansion + SCSI		8  Amp
	Color Frame Buffer		20 Amp
	Graphics Processor		20 Amp
	Sky Floating Point		4  Amp
	SMD via Multibus Adaptor	10 Amp
	Tape via Multibus Adaptor	6  Amp

Large (9 slots)				100 Amp

	CPU				12 Amp
	Memory Expansion + SCSI		8  Amp
	Color Frame Buffer		20 Amp
	Graphics Processor		20 Amp
	Sky Floating Point		4  Amp
	SMD via Multibus Adaptor	10 Amp
	Tape via Multibus Adaptor	6  Amp
	Spare I				10 Amp
	Spare II			10 Amp

* Maximize number of large Eurocard slots
   Minimum of 7 slots, 8 or 9 slots if feasible

* Maximize power supply
   100 Amps at +5V
   10 Amps at -5V
   2.5 Amps at +12V
   2.5 Amps at -12V

* Adapt Multibus boards via Adaptor cards

* Alternatives to Multibus boards:

   - VME Boards with equivalent function
   - SCSI controllers for SMD and 1/2" Tape
