<center>**SUN Microsystems, Inc. - Company Profile**</center>


SUN Microsystems, Inc. was formed in February 1982 with the mission to
develop, manufacture, market, and support high-performance
graphics workstations for the scientific/engineering, OEM,
and university market place.

SUN Microsystems is building on the work previously performed by its founders
at Stanford University and University of California in Berkeley
in personal workstations and network operating systems.
In addition, the company has assembled a team of highly skilled
professionals in the areas of UNIX, languages, graphics, and networks.
The company is supported by a growing operations and marketing staff.

The company was launched with a venture financing from two major
San Francisco Bay Area firms. Its founding members are:

Andreas Bechtolsheim, Director of Hardware Development,
designer of the SUN Workstation,
MS Computer Engineering Carnegie Mellon University,
PhD candidate in EE/CS Stanford University.

William N. Joy, Director of Software Development,
principal programmer and senior architect of the Berkeley UNIX system,
MS Computer Science UC Berkeley, PhD Candidate UC Berkeley.

Vinod Khosla, President,
formerly founder and Financial Officer of Daisy Systems,
MS Electrical Engineering Carnegie Mellon University,
MBA Stanford University.

Scott McNealy, Director of Operations,
formerly Director of Operations at Onyx Systems,
BA Harvard University, MBA Stanford University.

Other members of the technical staff include:

Peter Costello, formerly Software Engineer at Hewlett Packard.
BS and MS in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University.

John Gilmore, previously independent software consultant.

Tom Lyon, previously Communications Systems Architect at Amdahl,
BSEE from Princeton in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.

Vaughan Pratt, Professor of Computer Science at Stanford,
is a consultant to the company.

Martin Rattner, formerly Software Engineering Manager at National Semiconductor,
BS in Electrical Engineering from MIT and MS in Computer Science from Stanford.

Bill Shannon, formerly Senior Software Engineer in DEC's UNIX Engineering Group,
BS and MS in Computer Engineering from Case Western.

Mike Shantz, formerly Supervisor of the Software Products Group at DeAnza Systems,
MS in Biomedical Engineering from Drexel University,
PhD in Information Science from Cal-Tech.

Richard Tuck, formerly Software Engineer at Hewlett Packard,
MS in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science UC Berkeley.
